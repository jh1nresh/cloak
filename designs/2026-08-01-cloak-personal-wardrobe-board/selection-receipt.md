# Direction Selection Receipt

- Decision date: 2026-08-01
- Decision owner: founder
- Selected: B structure + A typography
- Rejected: A as a complete editorial shell; B's potentially decorative scrapbook treatment; C as the primary product metaphor.
- Reason: the product needs the personal warmth and wardrobe-memory visibility of B without losing the clarity, discipline, and fashion credibility of A.
- Risks accepted: garment cutouts require reliable isolation; sparse closets need a graceful fallback; strict composition limits are required to prevent visual clutter.
- Rules that must survive: Today uses vertically paged, one-look-per-viewport browsing; user remains the hero; Original / Me remains explicit; owned evidence is truthful; Save / Skip / Shop hierarchy remains clear; warmth does not come from a pink wash.
- Open questions: whether the final tab labels should be `Today / Closet / Add / Me` or `Discover / Closet / Add / Me`; whether handwritten personal notes belong in v1 or should wait for user-authored notes.
